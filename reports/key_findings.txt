Titanic Data Cleaning and EDA - Key Findings
=======================================================

1. The overall passenger survival rate was 38.38%.
2. Female passengers had a survival rate of 74.20%, compared with 18.89% for male passengers.
3. First-class passengers had a survival rate of 62.96%, while third-class passengers had a survival rate of 24.24%.
4. Children had a survival rate of approximately 57.97%.
5. Passengers travelling alone had a survival rate of 30.35%, compared with 50.56% for passengers travelling with family.
6. The median fare was 26.00 for survivors and 10.50 for non-survivors.
7. Passengers with recorded cabin information had a survival rate of 66.67%, compared with 29.99% for passengers without recorded cabin information.
8. Gender and passenger class showed some of the clearest relationships with survival. Fare, family size, age group, title, embarkation port, and cabin availability also showed noticeable patterns.
9. These relationships show association rather than direct causation. For example, fare and cabin information are also strongly connected to passenger class.
